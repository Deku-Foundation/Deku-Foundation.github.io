# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Deku Foundation aims to maintain respectful, constructive, accessible, and evidence-led open-source spaces. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help make them welcoming and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and be open to correction.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions focused on project goals, user safety, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sustained personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to repository maintainers through a verified contact channel listed on the official organization profile. Include links and relevant context, but avoid unnecessary sensitive information. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly where practical and may remove content or restrict participation proportionately.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, community channels, and public spaces when someone is representing the project.
